**Counterexamples/falsifications:**

**Surprise discovery (B2):** The multiplicative correlation inequality I(i,k) ≥ I(i,j)·I(j,k) is not just sufficient but *exactly equivalent* to the metric triangle inequality for d = -log(I). This means there is no weaker condition on I that still yields a metric — the multiplicative condition is both necessary and sufficient.
